# Insights & Recommendations

Detailed findings from the Google Merchandise Store channel performance analysis. Each finding follows the structure: what the data shows, why it matters, and what action is recommended.

---

## Finding 1: Referral is the most valuable channel

Referral ranks 4th for traffic with 104,838 sessions, yet ranks 1st for both revenue ($651,429.91, 42.3% of total) and conversion rate (5.08%, the highest of all channels).

This likely reflects third-party trust signals - visitors arriving from partner sites, review platforms, or recommended blogs are pre-qualified and arrive with higher purchase intent than cold traffic.

**Recommendation:** Identify and formalise relationships with high-authority partner websites and blogs already sending referral traffic. Explore additional channel partnerships or alliances with sites in similar categories, and discuss with existing partners whether expanded placements or content collaborations are possible.

---

## Finding 2: Social drives traffic but not revenue

Social is the second highest traffic channel with 226,117 sessions (25% of total), yet generates only 0.3% of total revenue and converts at 0.05% - the joint lowest of all channels.

This suggests Social is currently being measured against the wrong goal.

**Recommendation:** Refocus Social away from direct sales KPIs and toward generating Referral traffic, since Referral converts at 5.08% - the highest of all channels. Content that encourages bloggers, YouTubers, and industry websites to feature or recommend Google Merchandise Store products would seed high-intent traffic through a proven high-converting channel. In practice this means shifting Social KPIs toward brand mentions, content shares, and referral links generated. Trial a creator collaboration programme over 90 days and measure the downstream impact on Referral session volume and revenue.

---

## Finding 3: Display is underinvested relative to its efficiency

Display has the second lowest traffic of all channels (6,262 sessions) but ranks 4th for revenue ($78,337.46) and 2nd for conversion rate (2.28%) - the second highest of any channel.

This pattern suggests Display ads are highly targeted toward users with strong purchase intent, converting at nearly double the rate of Organic Search despite receiving a fraction of the traffic.

**Recommendation:** Increase Display budget incrementally and monitor conversion rate and revenue over the following months to validate whether the efficiency holds at greater scale. Even a modest increase in Display investment could yield disproportionate revenue gains given its current conversion performance.

---

## Finding 4: Paid Search shows the same pattern as Display

Paid Search ranks 6th for traffic but 3rd for conversion rate (1.85%), confirming the same pattern seen with Display - intent-driven channels consistently outperform high-volume channels on efficiency.

Despite this, Paid Search receives limited investment relative to its conversion potential.

**Recommendation:** Review current budget allocation for Paid Search and track ROAS month-on-month over the next quarter to validate whether increased spend produces proportional revenue gains, consistent with the Display finding.

---

## Finding 5: Referral and Direct dominate revenue throughout the year

Referral leads on monthly revenue in the majority of months, with Direct occasionally overtaking it - for example in February 2017 and October 2016. Both channels consistently outperform all others throughout the year, confirming their structural importance regardless of seasonal variation.

December 2016 shows a revenue peak across most channels, likely reflecting Christmas shopping behaviour. The most notable anomaly is Display in April 2017, where revenue jumped from near zero to $42,000 in a single month, suggesting a targeted campaign was launched or budget was significantly increased. August 2017 figures are incomplete, as the dataset ends mid-month, and are excluded from trend conclusions.

**Recommendation:** Investigate what drove the April 2017 Display spike - if it was a specific campaign, identify what made it successful and assess whether it can be replicated. Build seasonal planning around the consistent December revenue peak across channels.

---

## Finding 6: Direct traffic performance may be affected by non-commercial sessions

Direct is the 3rd largest traffic channel, 2nd for revenue, but only 4th for conversion rate (1.44%). 

This gap may be explained by non-commercial sessions within the Direct channel, such as internal staff visits and users who bookmarked pages during a consideration phase but were not yet ready to purchase. Revenue per converting session for Direct remains strong, suggesting the channel performs well once intent is present - the lower conversion rate may reflect traffic composition rather than channel weakness.

**Recommendation:** Ensure page load speed is optimised and the user experience is clear and frictionless, particularly for returning visitors who arrive with purchase intent. If feasible, consider segmenting internal/staff traffic from customer traffic in future analyses to get a clearer picture of true customer conversion rates for this channel.

---

## Finding 7: Organic Search has untapped potential as a free channel

Organic Search drives the most traffic of all channels (381,561 sessions, 42% of total) yet ranks 3rd for revenue and 5th for conversion rate (0.90%).

As a largely free channel, even modest conversion rate improvements would produce meaningful revenue gains at low cost.

**Recommendation:** Conduct a full SEO audit to identify underperforming pages. Build content aligned to each stage of the buyer journey - awareness, consideration, and decision. Ensure pages load quickly and develop a keyword strategy that accounts for AI-driven search behaviour, which is increasingly influencing how users find and evaluate products.

---

## Summary

The clearest opportunity from this analysis is a mismatch between investment and outcome across three channels. Social receives substantial traffic but produces almost no revenue, while Display and Paid Search receive minimal traffic but convert efficiently. Reallocating effort - both budget and strategic focus - from Social toward Referral-generating activities, while increasing investment in Display and Paid Search, represents the most direct path to improved overall return without requiring additional total spend.
